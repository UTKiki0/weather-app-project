      Weather data

      Forecasts and place search come from Open-Meteo.com, licensed CC BY 4.0. Open-Meteo's free service is for non-commercial use.

      Each day's numbers cover daytime hours, 8 am to 8 pm local time at the chosen place: temperature is the average, and chance of rain, wind, and UV are the highest values in that window. Temperatures are in °F and wind is in mph. Every day shown, including today, is a forecast.

      The sky description and background come from the same hours: the most common condition in the window, or the most severe rain, snow, or storm condition when the chance of rain is 50% or more.

      Methodology

      Values are rounded to whole numbers, then these rules pick the outfit and reminders.

      Outfit

      The outfit follows an effective temperature: the forecast temperature, minus 5°F when wind is 15 mph or more, or the chance of rain is 50% or more.

      • Cold: below 40°F
      • Cool: 40–55°F
      • Mild: above 55 to 70°F
      • Warm: above 70°F

      Based on common dressing guides such as Fit The Forecast and Nike. These are informal consumer guides, not a medical standard.

      Reminders

      • Umbrella: 50% or higher chance of precipitation.
      • Sunscreen: UV index 3 or higher, where the EPA UV Index Scale recommends sun protection.
      • Extra hydration: temperature of 85°F or higher. This threshold is a product choice, not a regulation.
      • Windy: wind of 25 mph or more. Official National Weather Service wind advisories start higher, at 31–39 mph sustained, as a safety level rather than an everyday-dressing one.
      Privacy practice

      Your location (a searched place or your device's position) is used only to ask Open-Meteo for weather. The app sends it to no other service.

      Your most recent location, and the outfit and reminder choices for each date you view, are saved only in this browser's local storage on your device. There are no accounts and no server-side storage of your data, and the app has no analytics.

      The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.
